Physique 57  ·  Brand Development & UX Design

Glow With
the Flow.

Overview

From Studio
to Screen.

Brand revitalization, transitioning from in-studio sessions to virtual classes. Physique 57 needed a visual system that could carry the energy of a live workout into a digital product, without losing what made the studio feel like the studio.

The Transition Challenge

A pandemic-driven shift to live streaming required an entirely new asset set and product experience, built on top of an existing brand identity rather than replacing it.

The Brand Constraint

The refresh had to feel energetic and modern without abandoning the signature studio personality that loyal members already connected with.

Approach

Glow With
the Flow

Inspired by the studio’s signature ambiance lighting, the refresh was themed “Glow With The Flow,” capturing the energy of the trainees and the glow of sweat and heat generated during intense workouts.

The design direction employed clean, modern lines throughout the visual system to unify the overall redesign and bridge physical and virtual experiences. Rather than reinventing the wheel, the emphasis was on creating the necessary assets to white-label an existing industry-standard tech solution, building around what already worked.

Mobile and live streaming were the primary surfaces. The brand refresh was designed to address the significant shift in how members were accessing content, with every touchpoint reconsidered for smaller screens and streaming contexts.

Refined Color Palette

Modernized to carry the signature studio energy across digital surfaces, vibrant without being harsh, refined without losing heat.

New Icon System

A consistent icon set built for cross-platform use, with app, web, and streaming surfaces all speaking the same visual language.

Custom Illustrations

Elevated storytelling across the product and brand, with illustrations designed to feel personal and energetic, not generic.

Updated Brand Guidelines

Documented for clarity and scalability, a system the team could hand off, extend, and apply consistently across every channel.

Solution

Refreshing
a Brand

The brand update delivered complete visual system revitalization. The new identity felt energetic, cohesive, and reflected the studio’s core personality, adapted for the digital-first world members were now living in.

Clean, modern lines unified the overall redesign across every surface. The white-label streaming integration meant working within technical constraints, and the design system was built to be flexible enough to apply consistently whether inside a custom-built product or wrapped around a third-party platform.

Outcomes

What the Refresh
Delivered

Visual System Revitalization

Complete brand refresh, energetic, cohesive, and aligned with the studio’s core personality across all digital touchpoints.

Cross-Platform Icon System

Consistent iconography deployed across app, web, and streaming surfaces, one language across every product surface.

Mobile-First Experience

Brand and product designed for the shift to mobile and live streaming, built to perform where members actually were.

Scalable Brand Guidelines

A documented system built for handoff, clear enough to extend consistently without the original designer in the room.
