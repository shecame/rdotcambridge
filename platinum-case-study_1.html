Overview

The Communication
Problem

Liberty Mutual's Asynchronous Comms and Comms Central teams were managing customer conversations across three separate tools. Reps had to utilize Word docs as manual templates for message responses, and leverage a separate external tool to verify customers.

Approach

Fragmented tools,
fractured experience

Before any design work began, I needed to understand the system. Workflow observation alongside reps revealed three disconnected tools, manual workarounds baked into daily habits, and no unified experience of what a conversation looked like from the agent’s side.

A heuristic evaluation across all three tools mapped failures in consistency and learnability. A landscape analysis of leading communication platforms benchmarked modern patterns in verification, templating, and surfaced an opportunity to leverage AI-assisted response.

The research surfaced a clear reframe: build the system around how agents actually work. To stay anchored throughout, I established design principles at the outset, giving the team a shared standard to pressure-test decisions against as the work evolved.

Handle Time

Reduce handle time and improve first-contact resolution.

Tool Consistency

Create consistency in rep-facing communication tools.

Brand Alignment

Align with Liberty Mutual brand voice and design system standards.

Scalable Foundation

Build a scalable foundation for future channel expansion.

Design Principles

Design
Principles

To keep cross-functional teams aligned and tradeoff decisions grounded, I conducted a short session to establish a set of principles before any feature work began. Design principles are value statements — the ones you return to when two valid directions are competing and someone has to make a call. For Platinum, that shared foundation kept decisions consistent, rationale clear, and debates short. When the tradeoff is difficult, the principle is what you lean on.

01
Start with the rep’s pain

If it doesn’t close a real workflow gap, it doesn’t belong.

02
Context before the conversation

Reps should know who they’re talking to before the thread opens.

03
The rep checks it before sending

Assistance without replacing judgment.

04
Keep the work in one place

No tool switching, no context lost.

05
Exceptions shouldn’t feel like exceptions

Every path through the product should feel equally resolved.

Interface Architecture

The Anatomy
of Platinum

Why a three-panel layout? A review of existing communication hubs and the current tool landscape pointed to a clear structural gap — reps were context-switching between three tools to manage a single conversation. The three-panel layout consolidates queue, thread, and recommendations into one workspace without sacrificing clarity.

Left Panel

Conversation queue with verification status surfaced inline. Reps see all active conversations at a glance without leaving context.

Center Panel

Full conversation thread: customer messages, rep responses, timestamps, media, and system alerts including opt-out notifications.

Right Panel

AI-generated message recommendations and templates. Architected as a flexible panel, additional tabs expand here as the platform grows.

Feature Design

Designed features,
deliberate decisions

Every feature traces back to a documented rep pain point. The design decisions follow from the workflow gaps, not the other way around.

Intelligent Recommendations, Rep-Controlled

The system generates contextually relevant message suggestions based on the conversation. Reps see the full message before accepting, not a truncated preview. High-confidence suggestions are surfaced at the top. Profile data autofills variables like name and policy details. Reps can edit before sending. The AI handles the starting point; the rep owns the send.

Conversation Thread Image Management

Customers submit images: vehicle damage, registration documents, ID, directly in the thread. Reps can expand, zoom in/out, save, and delete without leaving the conversation. No external tools, no downloads. The image stays in context, where the work is happening.

Automated by Default, Manual When Needed

Verification is automated against the customer’s phone number on file. When the number is unavailable or unmatched, reps can manually verify by searching the customer record directly within the platform. The flow handles both cases in the same interface, no tool-switching, no workflow break. Identity confidence is surfaced in the conversation queue so reps enter each conversation with context already established.

Impact & Outcomes
